The ONLY AI Desktop App You Need: Cherry Studio Workstation

AI & tools, explained like you are five.

Still jumping between dozens of AI tabs every single day? One tab for one chatbot, another for a translator, a third for an image tool — the "AI tab fatigue" problem is real, and it eats your focus. Cherry Studio Workstation is the open-source answer: one desktop app that replaces the pile of tabs with a single private powerhouse for Windows, macOS and Linux.

The scale is the first surprise. Cherry Studio runs 300+ frontier AI models — OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek — from one window, and supports massive 1-million-token memory windows, so a single conversation can hold an entire book instead of a few paragraphs. The chat stops forgetting; the context stops vanishing.

The game changer is side-by-side model debugging. Ask your question once and watch several models answer in parallel — then compare, judge, and pick the best response. Instead of trusting one chatbot's confidence, you see exactly where models disagree. For anyone working with AI seriously, this one feature changes the daily workflow.

Then there is the ready-made workforce: 1,000+ pre-configured specialized assistants, plus the ability to build your own knowledge bases from local files. Your documents, your notes, your rules — the assistant learns your material instead of guessing from the open internet.

And the principle underneath everything is privacy-first: 100% local data. Your chats and your knowledge bases are stored strictly on your own device — not on someone else's server. For a tool that touches your documents and your questions every day, that architecture is not a bonus feature; it is the point.

The price completes the argument: the community edition is completely free, and easy WebDAV sync keeps your setup consistent across devices. Chat, draw and translate in one all-in-one workspace — then ask yourself the video's closing question: what will you build first?

FAQ

What is Cherry Studio Workstation?

An open-source desktop AI app for Windows, macOS and Linux that unifies chat, drawing and translation in one privacy-first workspace — designed to replace the daily juggling of dozens of AI browser tabs.

Which AI models can it run?

More than 300 frontier models, including OpenAI, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek, with support for 1-million-token context windows — and you can compare multiple models' answers side by side.

How does it protect privacy?

Data is stored strictly on your local device: chats, files and knowledge bases never have to leave your computer. The privacy-first, 100%-local architecture is the app's founding principle.

What are the specialized assistants?

Over 1,000 pre-configured assistants for different tasks, plus your own custom knowledge bases built from local files — so an assistant can answer from your documents rather than generic internet knowledge.
