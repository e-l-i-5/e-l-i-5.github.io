The modern AI workflow, for most people, is chaos with a browser history: one tab for this model, another for that one, a third for the chatbot that "knows" your documents — and every tab quietly shipping your sensitive data to somebody else's cloud. The video opens with exactly that scene and then offers the counterproposal: Msty Studio, a workspace that combines local offline LLMs — via engines like Ollama, MLX and llama.cpp — and hosted cloud models into one powerful, air-gapped private workspace. Your tabs collapse into one app, and your secrets stay on your machine.

The trick is the Model Hub: local engines versus hosted providers, side by side in one place. Want the privacy of a model that never leaves your computer? Run it locally. Want the horsepower of a big cloud model for a heavy task? Reach for it — from the same window, under the same habits. The choice between private and powerful stops being a fork in the road and becomes a dropdown.

Where the studio gets interesting is what it lets the models do together. Crew Conversations deploy multi-agent collaboration — several personas debating and building in one thread. Split Chats run side-by-side split testing, the same prompt to different models, answers compared like parallel test tubes. Agent Mode executes multi-step tasks — a workflow, not just a reply. Add Custom Prompts, Personas and Reusable Skills, and a built-in Media Studio for image and video concepting, and the app starts to feel less like a chat client and more like a workshop.

The quiet headline feature for privacy-minded users is Knowledge Stacks: 100% private, local RAG. Retrieval-augmented generation — feeding the model your own documents as context — normally means uploading those documents somewhere. Here the stacks live directly on your machine, so "the AI that knows my files" and "the AI that never phones home" can finally be the same AI. For teams, Shared Governance adds SSO, role-based access control and audit logs, with desktop app setup or web team deployment.

So the walkthrough's promise is specific: stop juggling dozens of browser tabs and stop exposing sensitive data to the cloud — one workspace, local when you want silence, cloud when you want muscle, private by default. The only question left is the video's closing one: what will you build first?

FAQ

What is Msty Studio?

A private AI workspace that combines local offline LLMs (via Ollama, MLX, llama.cpp) and hosted cloud models in one application — replacing the tab-juggling workflow with a single, air-gapped-capable environment.

How does it keep data private?

Local models run offline on your machine, and Knowledge Stacks store your documents for 100% private local RAG — retrieval happens directly on your computer, so sensitive data never has to reach the cloud. Cloud models remain available when you choose them.

What are Crew Conversations and Split Chats?

Crew Conversations deploy multi-agent collaboration — several AI personas working in one conversation. Split Chats run the same prompt against different models side-by-side for instant comparison — split testing for AI.

What does it offer for teams?

Shared Governance: SSO, role-based access control (RBAC) and audit logs, plus desktop app setup or web team deployment — private AI that a whole organization can use and administer.
